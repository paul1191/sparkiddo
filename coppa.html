COPPA Notice

Our commitment to children's privacy under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective date: June 2026

🛡️ Sparkiddo is designed for children under 13

As a platform primarily directed at children aged 3–12, Sparkiddo complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), 15 U.S.C. § 6501 et seq., and the FTC's implementing Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 312.

Who This Applies To

Scope

This COPPA Notice supplements our Privacy Policy and applies specifically to personal information collected from or about children under the age of 13 in the United States.

Parental Consent

How we obtain verifiable parental consent

Children do not register for Sparkiddo directly. A parent or legal guardian must create an account using a valid email address before any child profile can be created. By creating a child profile, the parent or guardian provides verifiable consent for the limited data practices described in this notice.

Parents may revoke consent at any time by deleting their child's profile or closing their account entirely.

What We Collect From Children

Minimal data, collected by parents on behalf of children

We collect the following information about children, entered by the parent or guardian (not by the child directly):

  • A first name or nickname (optional — parent may enter any label)
  • Age group selection (3–5, 6–8, or 9–12) — used only to select age-appropriate content
  • Avatar choice (from a set of fixed emoji, not photos)

During gameplay, we automatically collect:

  • XP earned per session and game choices made (by outcome category, not specific content)
  • Which game was played and approximate time

We do NOT collect: children's email addresses, phone numbers, photos, precise location, device identifiers, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

How We Use It

Limited purposes only

Information about children is used only to:

  • Provide age-appropriate game content and track progress within the app
  • Show parents a Growth Map report of their child's progress
  • Maintain the daily mission system and XP belt progression

We do not use children's data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the Sparkiddo service. We do not sell children's data under any circumstances.

Third Parties

No third-party access to children's data

We do not disclose personal information collected from children to any third party, except:

  • Supabase — our database provider, for secure storage only
  • Law enforcement — only when required by law or to protect child safety

We do not share children's data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other commercial third parties.

Your Rights as a Parent

Access, correction, and deletion

At any time, you have the right to:

  • Review the personal information we have collected about your child
  • Correct inaccurate information via your account settings
  • Delete your child's profile and all associated data
  • Refuse to permit further collection of your child's data by closing your account

Exercising these rights will not affect your ability to continue using Sparkiddo with a different profile, or to re-register later.

To exercise these rights, email us at support@sparkiddo.com. We will respond within 30 days and will not require you to create an additional account to make a request.
