🛡️ Safety first

Our safety promise to you

Before anything else — here is exactly what Sparkiddo does and does NOT do.

🚫
Absolutely zero ads
No banner ads, no video ads, no sponsored content. Ever. Ads in children's apps are a revenue model we refuse to use — full stop.
💳
No purchases for kids
Children cannot make any purchases in Sparkiddo. All transactions go through adult-facing screens only. Kids just play.
🔒
No child data collected
Children never register or log in. We only store a first name and avatar emoji. No email, no location, no device ID from children.
📵
No social features
No chat, no friend lists, no sharing to social media, no external links. Sparkiddo is a closed, safe environment.
🌐
COPPA & DPDP compliant
Fully compliant with India's DPDP Act, US COPPA and EU GDPR. You can delete all data instantly from your parent dashboard.
🔐
PIN-protected parental controls
Your parent dashboard is protected by a 4-digit PIN and device biometrics. Children cannot access your settings.
🤝

Our commitment to you

Sparkiddo will never introduce ads, never sell user data, and never make children the target of any commercial activity. This isn't just policy — it's the reason Sparkiddo was built. We're parents too.

❓ Parent FAQ

Questions parents always ask

Is Sparkiddo really completely free? ▾
Yes — all 14 games are free forever. We'll never put a paywall on the games themselves. Sparkiddo Plus is an optional upgrade that adds multi-child profiles, detailed reports, offline play and push notifications — but it's never required to play.
Do I need to create an account for my child? ▾
No — guest mode lets anyone play all 14 games without an account. Create a parent account to save your child's XP, belts, streaks and pet progress across visits. Children never register themselves — only parents do.
What data do you collect about my child? ▾
Almost nothing. We store the child's first name and avatar emoji (both chosen by the parent), their XP score, games played and streak count. No email address, no location, no device identifier, no photos — nothing personal from children. Parent email is stored securely for account access.
Can my child accidentally spend money? ▾
No — there are no in-app purchases accessible to children. Any premium features require parent account login and a payment screen that children cannot access. Children play — parents manage.
Is there an age limit for Sparkiddo? ▾
Sparkiddo is designed for children aged 3–12. Parents must be 18+ to create an account. Children do not need their own accounts. The parent dashboard includes an age filter so you can restrict games to age-appropriate content for younger children.
How do I delete my account and data? ▾
Log into your parent dashboard → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all data immediately — no waiting period, no retention. You can also delete individual child profiles without closing your account.
Is there an app coming? ▾
Yes! The Sparkiddo iOS and Android app is in development. It will include offline play, push notifications to remind kids to maintain their streaks, full parental controls and multi-child management. Join the waitlist on our Upcoming page to be first to know.
